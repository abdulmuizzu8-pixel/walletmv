## Full description (Google Play and App Store)

WalletMV is the budget app made in the Maldives. Open it in the morning and see one clear number: what's safe to spend today.

TRACK EVERY RUFIYAA
• Add an expense in one tap with quick-add buttons for the ferry, coffee, lunch and more
• Keep cash, bank and card accounts in one place
• Everything in MVR first, with 25 currencies when you travel

STAY IN CONTROL
• Set a monthly budget and see what's left before you spend
• Get a warning at 90% of your budget
• Never miss a bill: electricity, water, internet and rent reminders with due dates

SAVE FOR WHAT MATTERS
• Savings goals for your next holiday, an emergency fund or a new phone
• Watch each goal fill up as you add to it

GROCERIES MADE EASY
• Keep a shopping list and the everyday items you buy again and again
• Share the list on WhatsApp, then log the shop as one expense

YOUR MEMBER DISCOUNT CARD
• Show your WalletMV card at partner shops, cafés and dive centres for member deals

FOR SHOPS: MVSHOP
• Point of sale, invoices, quotations and receipts
• Stock with low-stock alerts and reorder lists
• GST reports for MIRA, customers, suppliers and loyalty cards

PRIVATE AND SECURE
• Your data is saved to your own WalletMV account and only you can see it
• Lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your fingerprint
• Delete your account any time in Settings

Questions or problems? Message us on WhatsApp: +960 798 7770.
